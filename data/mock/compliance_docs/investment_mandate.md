# Investment Mandate — Excerpt from Trust Deed and Offering Document
## Meezan Islamic Fund (MIF) — Illustrative Compliance Reference Document

**Trustee:** Central Depository Company of Pakistan Limited (CDC)
**Management Company:** Al Meezan Investment Management Limited
**Fund Category:** Open End — Shariah Compliant Equity Scheme
**Governing Framework:** Non-Banking Finance Companies and Notified Entities Regulations, 2008, as amended, and directives issued by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) from time to time.

*Note: This is an illustrative compilation for testing purposes, built in the real clause style used in Al Meezan's actual, publicly available offering documents. Numeric thresholds below are stated explicitly (rather than only referencing "the Regulations," as real offering documents typically do) so that a compliance-checking agent can evaluate them directly.*

---

### Clause 4.1 — Investment Objective and Authorized Investments

The Fund shall primarily invest in Shariah Compliant equity securities listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange, focusing on both capital gains and dividend income, in accordance with the guidelines and screening criteria issued by the Shariah Advisor from time to time.

### Clause 5.1 — Minimum Equity Allocation

At least seventy percent (70%) of the Fund's Net Assets shall remain invested in listed equity securities during the year, based on quarterly average investment calculated on a daily basis. The remaining Net Assets shall be invested in cash and/or near-cash instruments.

### Clause 6.1 — Single Entity Exposure Limit

The Management Company (Wakeel) shall not, at any time, hold Investments in the equity securities of any single company exceeding **ten percent (10%)** of the Fund's Net Assets. If and so long as the value of the holding in a particular company shall exceed this limit, the Management Company shall not purchase any further Investments in such company, and shall take appropriate steps to bring the exposure back within the prescribed limit within the timeframe specified by the Regulations.

### Clause 6.2 — Single Sector Exposure Limit

The Management Company (Wakeel) shall not, at any time, hold Investments in the equity securities of companies within any single sector, as classified by the Pakistan Stock Exchange, exceeding **twenty percent (20%)** of the Fund's Net Assets. If and so long as the value of the holding in a particular sector shall exceed this limit, the Management Company shall not purchase any further Investments in such sector.

### Clause 6.3 — Shariah Compliance Restriction

All Investments of the Fund shall be in adherence to the Islamic Shariah, as advised by the Shariah Advisor. In particular, the Fund shall not invest in the equity securities of any company whose core business is conventional (non-Islamic) banking, conventional insurance, or any other activity deemed non-compliant by the Shariah Advisor, regardless of the size of the proposed holding. This restriction applies irrespective of the single entity and sector exposure limits stated in Clauses 6.1 and 6.2, and is not subject to any minimum threshold.

### Clause 7.1 — Minimum Liquidity Requirement

The Fund shall maintain a minimum of **five percent (5%)** of its Net Assets in cash or near-cash instruments at all times, to ensure the Fund's ability to meet redemption requests in the ordinary course of business.

### Clause 8.1 — Reporting and Trustee Oversight

The Trustee shall be furnished with such reports as are necessary to verify the Fund's continuing compliance with Clauses 5.1, 6.1, 6.2, 6.3, and 7.1, and any breach identified shall be reported to the Trustee and the Fund's Board without delay.
