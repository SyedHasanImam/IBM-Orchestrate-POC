# Regulatory Circular Excerpts — Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP)
## Reference Document: Master Circular — Mutual Funds / Collective Investment Schemes / Investment Advisory Services

*Source: SECP Master Circular (Specialized Companies Division, Policy Regulation and Development Department), consolidating circulars issued from January 06, 2009 to April 09, 2020. Excerpts below are drawn from Chapter 2 (Categorization of Collective Investment Schemes and Investment Avenues) and Chapter 3 (Disclosure Requirements), paraphrased for this reference set.*

---

### Excerpt 1 — Equity Scheme Categorization (Chapter 2, Clause 2.1)

*Source: SECP Circular No. 7 of 2009, dated March 06, 2009*

Under SECP's categorization framework for open-end Collective Investment Schemes, a scheme classified as an Equity Scheme must keep at least 70% of its Net Assets invested in listed equity securities during the year, based on a quarterly average calculated on a daily basis. The remaining Net Assets are to be held in cash and/or near-cash instruments, which include cash in bank accounts (excluding term deposit receipts) and treasury bills of no more than 90 days' maturity. Equity Schemes are not permitted to take exposure through the Margin Trading System.

### Excerpt 2 — Shariah Compliant (Islamic) Scheme Requirements (Chapter 2, Clause 2.5)

*Source: SECP letter no. SCD/AMCW/873/2014, dated February 10, 2014*

A Collective Investment Scheme categorized as Shariah Compliant (Islamic) is required to invest its assets only in Shariah-compliant assets, as determined by the Fund's Shariah Advisor. Each such scheme must also be separately categorized by its underlying asset class (equity, income, fund of funds, etc.) and must follow all conditions applicable to that underlying category in addition to the Shariah-compliance requirement.

### Excerpt 3 — Entity, Group, and Sector Exposure Limits (Reference: Regulation 55, NBFC Regulations, 2008)

*Referenced in Chapter 2, Clauses 2.14.1 and 2.17.3 of the Master Circular*

The Master Circular confirms that Collective Investment Schemes authorized to invest in equity securities are subject to entity, group, and sector exposure limits "as prescribed in the Regulations" — i.e., Regulation 55 of the Non-Banking Finance Companies and Notified Entities Regulations, 2008. Asset Management Companies must ensure ongoing compliance with these per-party, per-group, and sector limits for all equity and equity-oriented holdings, including any exposure taken through Real Estate Investment Trust units or exchange-traded equity future contracts.

*Note for this reference set: for testing purposes, the applicable single-entity limit is treated as 10% of Net Assets and the applicable single-sector limit is treated as 20% of Net Assets, consistent with the Fund's own Trust Deed (Clauses 6.1 and 6.2).*

### Excerpt 4 — Limit on Non-Eligible Equity Securities (Chapter 2, Clause 2.12)

*Source: SECP Circular No. 13 of 2017, dated April 28, 2017*

A Collective Investment Scheme's holdings in equity securities that fail specified eligibility criteria — including securities not eligible for the Central Depository System, securities not traded on the regular counter of the Pakistan Stock Exchange, companies with free float below 15% (or 50,000,000 shares, whichever is less), or companies with major non-compliance issues such as governance or audit qualifications — must not, in aggregate, exceed 10% of the Fund's overall equity portfolio on a monthly average basis.

### Excerpt 5 — Disclosure Requirements for Non-Compliant Investments (Chapter 3, Clause 3.2)

*Source: SECP Circular No. 16 of 2010, dated July 07, 2010*

Where a Collective Investment Scheme's portfolio is, in whole or in part, non-compliant with either its assigned category's investment criteria or the investment requirements of its own Constitutive Documents, the Asset Management Company must disclose this in the Offering Document, in advertisements, and in the Fund Manager Report and periodic financial statements. The prescribed disclosure format for non-compliant holdings requires the following fields for each affected investment:

| Name of Investment | Type of Investment | Value Before Provision | Provision Held | Value After Provision | % of Net Assets | % of Gross Assets |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Trustees are required to monitor compliance with this disclosure requirement on an ongoing basis and to report any non-compliance or deviation to the Commission in a timely manner.
